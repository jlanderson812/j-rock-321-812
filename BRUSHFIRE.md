# Brushfire

> "It does not take a majority to prevail, but rather a tireless, irate minority, 
> keen on setting brushfires of freedom in the hearts and minds of men."
> — Samuel Adams

## What is Brushfire?

Brushfire is a free and open source movement at the intersection of 
art, technology, and human empowerment.

It is not automation. It is empowerment.
It is not a product. It is a conviction.
It is not for the majority. It is for the tireless minority.

## Why does Brushfire exist?

Because the tool didn't exist. So we will build it.

For the artist who can't afford the $200.
For the artist who could afford it — and refuses.
For the curious. For the stubborn.
For the ones who look at impossible and ask: *how do I start?*

## Who is Brushfire for?

Leonardo Da Vinci. Samuel Adams. The kid with the CGI donut.
The sailor in the middle of the Pacific dreaming of something more.
The builder in Fortune 500 land waiting for a spark.
The student whose journey hasn't started yet.

You. Me. All of us.
